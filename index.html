Root-Cause Analysis

How current systems break down — and how we solve them.

01

WhatsApp Proxy Sharing

Problem in Static QR Systems

Students take a snapshot of static paper or projected QR codes and forward it to absent friends sleeping in hostels.

SmartAttend Solution

Dynamic token regenerates every 8 seconds. Screenshots expire before they can be forwarded and scanned.

02

Lecture Time Wastage

Problem in Traditional Roll Calls

Calling 60+ names wastes 10–15 minutes of an instructional 50-minute lecture, disrupting professor flow and student focus.

SmartAttend Solution

Simultaneous self-verification. All 60 students check in within 60 seconds while lecture starts.

03

Hostel & Canteen Scans

Problem in Simple Online Portals

Students click "Mark Present" from anywhere on campus without actually attending the physical classroom.

SmartAttend Solution

Classroom geofencing validates GPS coordinates within a 35m perimeter around the lecture hall.

04

Admit-Card Debarment Shocks

Problem in End-of-Term Registers

Attendance registers are compiled only days before finals. Students learn they are below 75% when it's too late to recover.

SmartAttend Solution

Real-time student radar with an interactive attendance recovery calculator updated continuously.
